Bauliver Uploader

A private publishing and reporting tool for one independent YouTube creator.

What it does

Bauliver Uploader runs on the creator's own computer and connects to the creator's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

  • Upload. Uploads videos the creator has finished and approved, with their title, description and schedule.
  • Reports. Reads the channel's own view, watch-time and subscriber statistics to build private performance reports.

What it does not do

  • It has no public sign-up and is used only by its operator.
  • It does not access other people's channels, comments or personal data.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
