Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Bauliver Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool operated by a single creator for use with their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what Google user data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data accessed

When the creator signs in with Google, the tool may access, only for the creator's own channel:

  • Permission to upload videos and set their metadata (scope youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl).
  • The channel's own videos, playlists and public statistics (scope youtube.readonly).
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, traffic sources and subscriber counts (scope yt-analytics.readonly).

2. How data is used

Only to upload videos the creator has approved and to produce private performance reports for the creator. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and security

  • All data stays on the creator's own computer.
  • OAuth tokens are stored in files readable only by the creator's operating-system user and are never written to logs.
  • Reports are local files that may be deleted at any time.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, transferred or shared with any third party, and is not used for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the creator revokes access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the tool then deletes its stored tokens. Reports are kept until the creator deletes them.
