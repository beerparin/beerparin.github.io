Bauliver Uploader

A personal publishing and reporting tool for one independent video creator.

What it does

The creator produces short videos and serialized stories (Thai-language storytelling, wuxia fiction and short entertainment clips). Bauliver Uploader runs on the creator's own computer and connects to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs.

  1. Upload to drafts. After the creator approves a finished video, the tool uploads it to the creator's TikTok inbox. The creator then opens TikTok, edits the caption if needed, and posts it themselves.
  2. Weekly report. The tool reads the creator's follower count and the view, like, comment and share counts of the creator's own videos, and builds a private weekly report showing which videos performed best.

What it does not do

  • It is not available to other users and has no public sign-up.
  • It never posts publicly without the creator's own action in the TikTok app.
  • It does not read other people's accounts, messages or personal data.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Login

The creator signs in with "Login with TikTok". Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
